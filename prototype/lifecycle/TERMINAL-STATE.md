# Terminal zero is a finalized observation

The record probe authenticates the validator's actual balance as zero at a specific finalized state after its finite exit and withdrawable epochs have been reached. It does not promise that the validator or receiving contract can never receive further cash.

The RPC label `withdrawal_done` uses **effective balance** zero, after the withdrawable epoch. Effective balance is separate from the actual balance list. Therefore the lifecycle observer also requires an explicit actual balance of zero, and the record contract verifies the balance-list Merkle proof. Source: pinned Qrysm [validator status helper](https://github.com/cyyber/qrysm/blob/9b57ae0bbfba13e5f4d9cb0b7c6a45dd02a36de3/beacon-chain/rpc/qrl/helpers/validator_status.go#L63).

Under the reviewed eight-epoch committee period and sixteen-epoch withdrawal delay, the ordinary reward tail ends before withdrawability. Sync rewards are credited to assigned seats without a second active-status check. However, a next committee selected at boundary epoch S uses validators active at S and serves epochs S+8 through S+15. For exit epoch E, any such selection has S<E, so the last possible ordinary sync assignment ends by E+14. Withdrawability starts at E+16 or later. This follows the actual [selection](https://github.com/cyyber/qrysm/blob/9b57ae0bbfba13e5f4d9cb0b7c6a45dd02a36de3/beacon-chain/core/altair/sync_committee.go#L101), [rotation](https://github.com/cyyber/qrysm/blob/9b57ae0bbfba13e5f4d9cb0b7c6a45dd02a36de3/beacon-chain/core/altair/epoch_spec.go#L23) and [exit scheduling](https://github.com/cyyber/qrysm/blob/9b57ae0bbfba13e5f4d9cb0b7c6a45dd02a36de3/beacon-chain/core/validators/validator.go#L93) code.

Attestation reward eligibility requires activity in the previous epoch, or a slashed validator that is not yet withdrawable. Proposer and whistleblower rewards go to the current active proposer. These ordinary paths do not require an additional reward-tail wait after this controlled voluntary exit has produced an actual full withdrawal and a finalized zero-balance observation. Sources: [attestation eligibility](https://github.com/cyyber/qrysm/blob/9b57ae0bbfba13e5f4d9cb0b7c6a45dd02a36de3/beacon-chain/core/altair/epoch_precompute.go#L298), [proposer selection](https://github.com/cyyber/qrysm/blob/9b57ae0bbfba13e5f4d9cb0b7c6a45dd02a36de3/beacon-chain/core/helpers/validators.go#L308) and [slashing reward destination](https://github.com/cyyber/qrysm/blob/9b57ae0bbfba13e5f4d9cb0b7c6a45dd02a36de3/beacon-chain/core/validators/validator.go#L168).

Subsequent deposits to an existing validator key can increase its balance even after exit, because the existing-key [deposit path](https://github.com/cyyber/qrysm/blob/9b57ae0bbfba13e5f4d9cb0b7c6a45dd02a36de3/beacon-chain/core/blocks/deposit.go#L207) applies the top-up without changing the canonical recipient or exit state. Externally routed execution cash can also reach a contract later. The cash probe remains able to distribute later receipts by its immutable rules. A complete drain means reconciliation and zero contract cash at the measured boundary, with subsequent unsolicited cash handled separately.

This conclusion is specific to the pinned source and the reviewed eight/sixteen-epoch configuration. It does not generalize to a longer committee period or shorter withdrawal delay. The source review added no upstream change, replay experiment or synthetic reward transition.
